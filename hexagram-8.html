8. 比 – Bǐ (Holding Together)

Trigrams

☵☷ – Water above, Earth below

Meaning

Represents unity, alliance, and the bonds that hold people together. Like water on the earth, people naturally seek to join together around a worthy center. Cooperation and loyalty bring mutual benefit and security.

Interpretation

  • Seek alliances and build genuine relationships around a worthy cause or leader.
  • Examine your own sincerity before asking for the loyalty of others.
  • Those who hesitate too long to join will be left behind.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

有孚比之，无咎；有孚盈缶，终来有它，吉。

English: Holding together with sincerity, no blame. Sincerity fills the simple vessel; in the end another good thing comes. True loyalty, even from humble origins, draws good fortune.

Luck Level: Good

2nd Yao (二爻)

比之自内，贞吉。

English: Holding together from within. Persistence brings good fortune. Union that grows from inner conviction and genuine feeling is stable and lasting.

Luck Level: Good

3rd Yao (三爻)

比之匡人。

English: Holding together with the wrong people. Associating with those who lack virtue or integrity leads to misfortune. Choose companions carefully.

Luck Level: Neutral

4th Yao (四爻)

外比之，贞吉。

English: Holding together with those outside. Perseverance brings good fortune. Forming alliances beyond one's immediate circle is beneficial when done with integrity.

Luck Level: Good

5th Yao (五爻)

显比，王用三驱，失前禽，邑人不訡，吉。

English: Manifest unity. The king hunts from three sides, letting some game escape. The townspeople need not beware. Good fortune. A generous and open-hearted leader wins loyal followers.

Luck Level: Good

6th Yao (上爻)

比之无首，凶。

English: Holding together without a leader. Misfortune. A group without central direction and vision cannot hold together and will fall apart.

Luck Level: Misfortune
