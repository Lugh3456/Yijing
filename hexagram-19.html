Trigrams

☷☱ – Earth above, Lake below

Meaning

Represents approach and advance. The great comes close to the small with benevolent intention. A time of increasing influence and favorable growth. However, within the growth lies the seed of future difficulty — awareness of change ahead is wise.

Interpretation

  • This is a favorable time for advancement, influence, and reaching out to others.
  • Lead with genuine care and wisdom, not just authority.
  • Even in times of growth, remain mindful that conditions will eventually change.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

和临，贞吉。

English: Approaching with harmony — perseverance brings good fortune. A gentle and harmonious approach to others creates a solid foundation.

Luck Level: Good

2nd Yao (二爻)

和临，吉，无不利。

English: Approaching with harmony — good fortune, nothing is unfavorable. Warm, genuine engagement with others brings universal benefit.

Luck Level: Good

3rd Yao (三爻)

甘临，无攸利；既忧之，无咎。

English: Sweet-talking one's approach brings no benefit. But if one recognizes this and becomes anxious, no real harm results. False charm alone is not enough; genuine concern must follow.

Luck Level: Neutral

4th Yao (四爻)

至临，无咎。

English: Approaching wholeheartedly — no blame. Going the full distance with complete commitment and engagement.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

知临，大君之宜，吉。

English: Wise approach — this is right for the great ruler. Good fortune. Wise and knowing leadership that anticipates needs and acts with insight.

Luck Level: Good

6th Yao (上爻)

敦临，吉，无咎。

English: Sincere and devoted approach — good fortune, no blame. The most generous and wholehearted form of engagement brings lasting good.

Luck Level: Good
