57. 巽 – Xùn (The Gentle)

← Back to Home
← 56. 旅 58. 兑 →

Trigrams

☴☴ – Wind above, Wind below

Meaning

Represents the gentle, penetrating power of wind. Wind doubled — gentle yet persistent penetration. Like the wind that bends the grass without forcing it, the gentle approach achieves what brute force cannot. Consistent, quiet persistence reaches everywhere.

Interpretation

  • Achieve your goals through gentle, persistent influence rather than force.
  • Be flexible and adaptable; yield when necessary but never lose direction.
  • The gentle approach penetrates where direct force would be deflected.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

进退，利武人之贞。

English: Advancing and retreating — beneficial for the determination of a warrior. A certain decisiveness and courage is needed to hold one's gentle direction.

Luck Level: Neutral

2nd Yao (二爻)

巽在床下，用史巫窺若，吉，无咎。

English: Penetrating beneath the bed, using shamans and diviners in great numbers — good fortune, no blame. Gentle penetration through subtle and spiritual means reaches even hidden problems.

Luck Level: Good

3rd Yao (三爻)

频巽，吝。

English: Frequent and repetitive gentleness — regret. Over-repetition of the gentle approach without decisiveness loses effectiveness.

Luck Level: Caution

4th Yao (四爻)

悔亡，田获三品。

English: Regret disappears — the hunt catches three kinds of game. Patient and persistent pursuit through the gentle approach finally yields abundant results.

Luck Level: Good

5th Yao (五爻)

贞吉，悔亡，无不利，无初有终；先庚三日，后庚三日，吉。

English: Perseverance brings good fortune, regret disappears, nothing is unfavorable. No beginning but there is an end. Three days before and three days after the change — good fortune. Careful timing around turning points yields the best results.

Luck Level: Good

6th Yao (上爻)

巽在床下，丧其资斧，贞凶。

English: Penetrating beneath the bed — loses axe and resources. Persistence brings misfortune. Excessively subtle or secretive maneuvering ultimately costs more than it gains.

Luck Level: Misfortune
