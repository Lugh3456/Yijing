13. 同人 – Tóng Rén (Fellowship)

← Back to Home
← 12. 否 14. 大有 →

Trigrams

☰☲ – Heaven above, Fire below

Meaning

Represents community, fellowship, and the union of people under a shared purpose. True fellowship is open and impartial, not based on cliques or exclusion. When people come together in sincerity and without selfishness, great things can be accomplished.

Interpretation

  • Build alliances based on shared principles, not personal gain.
  • Open fellowship — not exclusive cliques — is the basis of real strength.
  • Temporary separation or conflict gives way to reunion and celebration.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

同人于门，无咎。

English: Fellowship at the gate — no blame. Opening yourself to connection with those who approach brings no harm. An open and welcoming attitude invites positive relationships.

Luck Level: Neutral

2nd Yao (二爻)

同人于宗，吝。

English: Fellowship only within the clan, regret. Limiting your circle to one's own group creates exclusiveness and invites conflict with those outside.

Luck Level: Caution

3rd Yao (三爻)

伏戎于莒，升其高陵，三岁不兴。

English: Soldiers hidden in the thicket, ascending the high mound — for three years they cannot rise. Secret hostility and suspicion prevent progress for a long time.

Luck Level: Caution

4th Yao (四爻)

乘其墓，弗克攻，吉。

English: Mounting the city wall, but unable to attack — good fortune. Realizing that the attack cannot succeed and withdrawing is wise. Restraint here leads to a better outcome.

Luck Level: Good

5th Yao (五爻)

同人，先号唱而后笑，大师克相遇。

English: In fellowship, first weeping and wailing, then laughing. After great effort, the armies meet. After long separation and struggle, reunion and harmony are achieved.

Luck Level: Good

6th Yao (上爻)

同人于郊，无悔。

English: Fellowship in the open country — no regret. Broad and open fellowship, beyond any specific group or agenda, brings no cause for regret.

Luck Level: Neutral
