31. 咏 – Xián (Influence)

← Back to Home
← 30. 离 32. 恒 →

Trigrams

☱☶ – Lake above, Mountain below

Meaning

Represents mutual attraction and the power of genuine influence. Lake rests on mountain — soft above, still below. True influence flows naturally from inner sensitivity and receptivity. The image is courtship and the power of genuine feeling to draw hearts together.

Interpretation

  • Open yourself to genuine connection and mutual influence.
  • True attraction is rooted in inner emptiness and receptivity, not manipulation.
  • Cultivate sensitivity to others and respond naturally from the heart.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

咏其拇。

English: Influence felt in the big toe. The first stirring of feeling, barely noticed, at the very base of the body.

Luck Level: Neutral

2nd Yao (二爻)

咏其腔，凶；居吉。

English: Influence in the calves — misfortune if one acts on it. Staying still is auspicious. Feeling the impulse to move but wisdom lies in not rushing forward.

Luck Level: Caution

3rd Yao (三爻)

咏其股，执其随，往吝。

English: Influence in the thighs, holding to what follows — going forward brings regret. Being swept along by impulse without reflection leads to regret.

Luck Level: Caution

4th Yao (四爻)

贞吉，悔亡；憧憧往来，朋从尔思。

English: Perseverance brings good fortune, regret disappears. Restless coming and going, and friends follow your thought. Centered sincerity naturally attracts like-minded companions.

Luck Level: Good

5th Yao (五爻)

咏其脖，无悔。

English: Influence in the back of the neck — no regret. The influence is steady and centered in the spine; not reactive, but sustained.

Luck Level: Neutral

6th Yao (上爻)

咏其輔颊舌。

English: Influence in the jaws, cheeks, and tongue. Influence through words and speech — the most surface level of connection.

Luck Level: Neutral
