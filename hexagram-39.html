39. 蹇 – Jiǎn (Obstruction)

← Back to Home
← 38. 睽 40. 解 →

Trigrams

☵☶ – Water above, Mountain below

Meaning

Represents obstruction and the difficulty of forward progress. Water on the mountain — danger above, stillness below. This is a time to pause, seek assistance, and consider carefully which direction to go. Looking inward and strengthening virtue is more productive than forcing ahead.

Interpretation

  • When the way forward is blocked, stop and reassess rather than forcing ahead.
  • Seek the help of wise and capable allies to navigate the obstruction.
  • Use the time of obstruction to cultivate inner virtue and prepare.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

往蹇，来誉。

English: Going forward leads to obstruction; coming back brings praise. Returning and waiting is rewarded; pushing forward is not the answer yet.

Luck Level: Neutral

2nd Yao (二爻)

王臣蹇蹇，匡躬之故。

English: The king's minister faces obstruction upon obstruction, but not for personal reasons. Enduring difficulty in loyal service to a higher cause, not for self-interest.

Luck Level: Neutral

3rd Yao (三爻)

往蹇，来反。

English: Going forward leads to obstruction; coming back turns things around. Return brings those dependent on you back to safety.

Luck Level: Neutral

4th Yao (四爻)

往蹇，来连。

English: Going forward leads to obstruction; coming back gathers allies. Retreat draws together those who are connected to you.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

大蹇，朋来。

English: Great obstruction — friends come. Facing the greatest difficulty, the right allies gather in response.

Luck Level: Caution

6th Yao (上爻)

往蹇，来硕，吉，利见大人。

English: Going forward leads to obstruction; coming back brings great achievement. Good fortune — beneficial to see the great person. The greatest return of all, bringing substantial gains and wise counsel.

Luck Level: Good
