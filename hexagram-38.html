Trigrams

☲☱ – Fire above, Lake below

Meaning

Represents opposition, estrangement, and difference. Fire moves upward; lake moves downward — they pull apart. Yet even in opposition there are moments of unexpected connection. Small matters can be handled in times of estrangement; great undertakings must wait.

Interpretation

  • In times of misunderstanding, focus on small shared tasks rather than grand ambitions.
  • Apparent opposites often contain unexpected points of connection.
  • Do not allow estrangement to harden into permanent division.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

悔亡；丧马，勿逐，自复；见恶人，无咎。

English: Regret disappears. The horse is lost — do not pursue it, it will return on its own. See the evil person — no blame. Even meeting with adversarial people has its purpose; be open.

Luck Level: Neutral

2nd Yao (二爻)

遇主于巷，无咎。

English: Meeting the ruler in a back lane — no blame. An unexpected, informal encounter with the right person brings no harm and may lead to good connection.

Luck Level: Neutral

3rd Yao (三爻)

见舆曳，其牛攀，其人天且勘，无初有终。

English: Seeing the cart dragged back, the ox halted, the person with a shaved head and mutilated nose — no good beginning but a good ending. The worst apparent circumstances sometimes resolve into good.

Luck Level: Neutral

4th Yao (四爻)

睽孤，遇元夫，交孚，厉无咎。

English: Isolated in opposition, but meeting a noble person — exchanging trust. Danger but no blame. A genuine encounter that bridges opposition is worth the difficulty.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

悔亡，噕宗噌肤，往何咎。

English: Regret disappears — one's companion bites through the skin to reach one. What regret is there in going forward? True connection breaks through the barriers of estrangement.

Luck Level: Neutral

6th Yao (上爻)

睽孤，见豕负涂，载鬼一车；先张之弧，后说之弧；匡寇婚婴，往遇雨则吉。

English: Isolated in opposition, seeing a pig covered in mud and a cartload of demons — first drawing the bow, then putting it down. Not a robber but a suitor. Going forward and meeting rain brings good fortune. What seemed monstrous resolves into a genuine meeting of hearts.

Luck Level: Good
