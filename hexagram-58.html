58. 兑 – Duì (The Joyous)

Trigrams

☱☱ – Lake above, Lake below

Meaning

Represents joy, delight, and the pleasure of genuine connection. Lake upon lake — joy renewing itself. True joy comes from inner sincerity and shared delight in virtue. It is the most attractive and magnetic of all qualities, drawing people together naturally.

Interpretation

  • Cultivate genuine inner joy and share it freely with others.
  • True joy comes from virtue and shared harmony, not from external stimulation.
  • Joyful openness and warmth attract the trust and loyalty of others.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

和兑，吉。

English: Harmonious joy — good fortune. Joy that is balanced, natural, and shared — the finest and most genuine expression of delight.

Luck Level: Good

2nd Yao (二爻)

孚兑，吉，悔亡。

English: Sincere joy — good fortune, regret disappears. Joy rooted in genuine inner sincerity dissolves all causes for regret.

Luck Level: Good

3rd Yao (三爻)

来兑，凶。

English: Coming joy — misfortune. Seeking pleasure from outside, always chasing after external stimulation, leads to misfortune.

Luck Level: Misfortune

4th Yao (四爻)

商兑未宁，介疾有喜。

English: Negotiated joy, not yet settled — controlling an ailment brings joy. Working through conflict and tension toward resolution, dealing with difficulties honestly, brings genuine satisfaction.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

孚于剔，有厉。

English: Trusting in what strips away — there is danger. Joy that depends on flatterers or those who strip away virtue is dangerous.

Luck Level: Neutral

6th Yao (上爻)

引兑。

English: Seductive joy. Being drawn in by enticing pleasures; the allure of what leads one astray.

Luck Level: Neutral
