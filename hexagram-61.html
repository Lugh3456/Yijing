Trigrams

☴☱ – Wind above, Lake below

Meaning

Represents the power of inner sincerity to transform and connect. Wind above the lake — the wind moves the surface of the water, inner truth moves others. Genuine sincerity penetrates even the hardest hearts. Like the crane calling to its young, a true call resonates across all distance.

Interpretation

  • Cultivate genuine inner sincerity as your greatest power.
  • True words spoken from the heart can move even those who seem unmovable.
  • Inner truth is the foundation of all meaningful relationships and influence.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

虚吉，有它不燕。

English: Inner quiet and openness — good fortune. Having other things is not restful. True sincerity comes from an empty, receptive inner space.

Luck Level: Good

2nd Yao (二爻)

鸣鹤在阴，其子和之；我有好爵，吾与尔黜之。

English: The calling crane in the shade — its young responds. I have a fine cup of wine — I will share it with you. The resonance of genuine inner truth reaches across all distance and barrier.

Luck Level: Neutral

3rd Yao (三爻)

得敌，或鼓或罢，或泣或歌。

English: Finding one's counterpart — sometimes drumming, sometimes stopping; sometimes weeping, sometimes singing. The encounter of genuine inner truth creates intense and varied emotions.

Luck Level: Neutral

4th Yao (四爻)

月几望，马匹亡，无咎。

English: The moon is nearly full — the horse companion is lost. No blame. At the peak of connection, sometimes the partner is released; this is natural and blameless.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

有孚栳如，无咎。

English: Holding sincerity as if bound — no blame. Inner truth that is firmly and completely held, like a binding, brings no fault.

Luck Level: Neutral

6th Yao (上爻)

翰音登于天，贞凶。

English: Cockcrow ascending to heaven — persistence brings misfortune. Announcing inner truth too loudly or in the wrong context; words that claim more than they can deliver.

Luck Level: Misfortune
