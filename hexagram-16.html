Trigrams

☳☷ – Thunder above, Earth below

Meaning

Represents joyful readiness and enthusiastic preparation. Thunder emerges from the earth with great energy. This is a time for inspiring others, establishing leaders, and setting things in motion. But enthusiasm must be channeled wisely to avoid complacency.

Interpretation

  • Build enthusiasm and inspire collective energy toward a worthy cause.
  • Establish clear leadership and organize before advancing.
  • Beware of indulgent or excessive enthusiasm that becomes laziness or self-deception.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

鸣豫，凶。

English: Proclaiming enthusiasm — misfortune. Bragging about one's excitement and anticipation before having done anything of substance brings trouble.

Luck Level: Misfortune

2nd Yao (二爻)

介于石，不终日，贞吉。

English: Firm as a rock, not waiting until the day is done — persistence brings good fortune. Decisiveness that does not wait for perfect conditions is rewarded.

Luck Level: Good

3rd Yao (三爻)

盱豫，悔；迟有悔。

English: Enthusiasm from looking upward in dependence brings regret. Hesitating also brings regret. Neither flattering dependence nor endless delay serves well.

Luck Level: Caution

4th Yao (四爻)

由豫，大有得；勿疑，朋盍簪。

English: The source of enthusiasm — great gain. No doubts; friends gather like hair in a clasp. True enthusiasm that draws from inner conviction brings allies and great results.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

贞疾，恒不死。

English: Persistence in illness, but not dying. Chronic difficulty that is endured without giving up — the situation is unpleasant but not fatal.

Luck Level: Neutral

6th Yao (上爻)

冥豫，成有渝，无咎。

English: Deluded enthusiasm — but there is a change. No blame. Even one who has gone too far in indulgent enthusiasm can correct course and find no lasting harm.

Luck Level: Neutral
