Trigrams

☴☷ – Wind above, Earth below

Meaning

Represents careful observation, reflection, and the power of example. The wind moves over the earth, touching everything. Through deep contemplation of oneself and the world, wisdom grows. A great leader rules as much by example as by command.

Interpretation

  • Take time for deep observation before acting; understand situations fully.
  • Examine your own life and conduct as carefully as you observe others.
  • The example set by those in authority shapes the entire community.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

童观，小人无咎，君子吝。

English: Childish contemplation — for a small person no blame, but for a noble person regret. A superficial view of life is acceptable for the uninitiated but insufficient for one of greater responsibility.

Luck Level: Caution

2nd Yao (二爻)

窥观，利女贞。

English: Peeping through a crack — favorable for women to persevere. A limited, partial view of things. Acceptable for someone in a sheltered position, but not for those needing broader perspective.

Luck Level: Neutral

3rd Yao (三爻)

观我生，进退。

English: Contemplating my own life — deciding whether to advance or retreat. Self-examination leads to a critical decision about one's direction.

Luck Level: Neutral

4th Yao (四爻)

观国之光，利用宾于王。

English: Contemplating the glory of the kingdom — it is beneficial to be a guest at the king's court. Observing good governance and aligning with excellence.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

观我生，君子无咎。

English: Contemplating my own life — the noble person is without blame. Honest self-examination, leading to a life of integrity, brings no cause for regret.

Luck Level: Neutral

6th Yao (上爻)

观其生，君子无咎。

English: Contemplating others' lives — the noble person is without blame. Observing how others live and conduct themselves, reflecting on the lessons, keeps the wise person blameless.

Luck Level: Neutral
