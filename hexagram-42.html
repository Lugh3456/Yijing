Trigrams

☴☳ – Wind above, Thunder below

Meaning

Represents increase, growth, and abundance flowing from above to below. The upper decreases to benefit the lower — a natural, generous outpouring. This is a propitious time for great undertakings; take action while the favorable conditions last.

Interpretation

  • This is an auspicious time for bold action and significant undertakings.
  • Share benefits generously downward to those who need them.
  • Act while the favorable conditions are present; the moment will pass.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

利用为大作，元吉，无咎。

English: Beneficial to undertake great works — primary good fortune, no blame. A time of genuine increase that supports large and ambitious undertakings.

Luck Level: Excellent

2nd Yao (二爻)

或益之，十朋之龟弗克违，永贞吉，王用享于帝，吉。

English: Someone increases it; ten pairs of tortoise shells cannot oppose it. Lasting perseverance brings good fortune. The king makes offering to God — good fortune. Benefiting all below is in harmony with heaven's own intention.

Luck Level: Good

3rd Yao (三爻)

益之用凶事，无咎，有孚中行，告公用圭。

English: Increased through calamitous events — no blame. Sincerity through the middle way, reporting to the duke with a jade tablet. Even from crisis comes growth when handled with integrity.

Luck Level: Misfortune

4th Yao (四爻)

中行，告公从，利用为依迁国。

English: Walking the middle way, reporting to the duke — following. Beneficial to use this for a capital transfer. The moment is right for major reorganization and expansion.

Luck Level: Neutral

5th Yao (五爻)

有孚惠心，勿问元吉；有孚惠我德。

English: Sincerely benevolent in heart — great good fortune without question. Sincerely bringing benefit to my virtue. The highest form of increase — enriching inner virtue and sharing it generously.

Luck Level: Excellent

6th Yao (上爻)

莫益之，或击之，立心勿恒，凶。

English: No one benefits it; someone may strike it. One's heart cannot maintain its course — misfortune. A heart that is not genuinely committed to benefiting others, and whose constancy is uncertain, invites attack and disaster.

Luck Level: Misfortune
