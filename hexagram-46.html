46. 升 – Shēng (Pushing Upward)

Trigrams

☷☴ – Earth above, Wind below

Meaning

Represents steady upward growth from below. The tree grows from the earth, pushing upward with patient, organic power. This is a time for confident advance. The growth is gradual but unstoppable when aligned with natural order and virtuous effort.

Interpretation

  • Advance steadily and confidently — conditions support your growth.
  • Work through proper channels and seek the guidance of those in authority.
  • Consistent, patient effort accumulates into significant achievement.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

允升，大吉。

English: Pushing upward with consent and trust — great good fortune. When one's upward progress is welcomed and supported, success is certain.

Luck Level: Excellent

2nd Yao (二爻)

孚乃利用穡，无咎。

English: Sincerity makes even simple offerings beneficial — no blame. With genuine inner sincerity, even the most modest offering has genuine efficacy.

Luck Level: Neutral

3rd Yao (三爻)

升虚邑。

English: Pushing upward into an empty city. Advancing without resistance into a place vacated by others — the way is wide open.

Luck Level: Neutral

4th Yao (四爻)

王用亨于岐山，吉，无咎。

English: The king makes offering at Mount Qi — good fortune, no blame. Performing sacred duties in a significant and historic place; connecting with deeper roots.

Luck Level: Good

5th Yao (五爻)

贞吉，升阶。

English: Perseverance brings good fortune — ascending the stairs. Step by step, steady advancement, each level attained through proper effort.

Luck Level: Good

6th Yao (上爻)

冥升，利于不息之贞。

English: Pushing upward in darkness — beneficial to be tireless in persistence. Even advancing without clear vision, the practice of unwearying constancy brings benefit.

Luck Level: Neutral
