About Yijing and Bagua

The Yijing (I Ching), also known as the Book of Changes, is an ancient Chinese text that has guided scholars, philosophers, and rulers for thousands of years. It is used for divination, moral reflection, and philosophical insight, offering guidance through the interpretation of hexagrams formed by six-line patterns.

The Bagua represents the 8 trigrams that form the foundation of all 64 hexagrams. Each trigram has its own symbolism, attributes, and natural associations, providing a framework to understand change, energy, and balance in the universe.

The 8 Trigrams (Bagua)

☰ 乾 Qian – Heaven

Creative, strong, forceful

☷ 坤 Kun – Earth

Receptive, nurturing, yielding

☳ 震 Zhen – Thunder

Movement, initiative, arousal

☴ 巽 Xun – Wind

Pervasive, gentle, penetration

☵ 坎 Kan – Water

Danger, depth, flow

☲ 離 Li – Fire

Clinging, light, clarity

☶ 艮 Gen – Mountain

Stillness, stability, obstruction

☱ 兌 Dui – Lake

Joyful, open, receptive
