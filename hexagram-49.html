49. 革 – Gé (Revolution)

← Back to Home
← 48. 井 50. 鼎 →

Trigrams

☱☲ – Lake above, Fire below

Meaning

Represents fundamental change, revolution, and transformation. Fire within the lake — two incompatible forces that transform each other. True revolution is only justified when the old order has become genuinely untenable and the new serves the genuine good of all.

Interpretation

  • Major transformation is sometimes necessary when the old order no longer serves.
  • Revolution must be carried out at the right time, with the right people, for the right reasons.
  • After transformation, consolidate and make the new order clear to all.

Six Yao (Lines) with Classical Quotes

1st Yao (初爻)

巩用黄牛之革。

English: Bound with yellow ox-hide. The time for revolution has not yet come; restraint and waiting are required before action.

Luck Level: Neutral

2nd Yao (二爻)

已日乃革之，征吉，无咎。

English: When the day comes, then make the revolution — going forward is auspicious, no blame. Waiting for the right moment and then acting decisively and correctly.

Luck Level: Good

3rd Yao (三爻)

征凶，贞厉；革言三就，有孚。

English: Going forward is misfortune, persistence is dangerous. But when the words of revolution have been confirmed three times, there is sincerity. Do not rush; let the case for change be made thoroughly.

Luck Level: Misfortune

4th Yao (四爻)

悔亡，有孚改命，吉。

English: Regret disappears — with sincerity, the mandate is changed. Good fortune. The genuine transformation of authority, supported by the people's trust, succeeds.

Luck Level: Good

5th Yao (五爻)

大人虎变，未占有孚。

English: The great person changes like a tiger — even before divining, there is trust. A truly great leader's transformation is so clear and powerful that it inspires confidence without requiring proof.

Luck Level: Neutral

6th Yao (上爻)

君子豹变，小人革面；征凶，居贞吉。

English: The noble person changes like a leopard; the petty person only changes their face. Going forward is misfortune; staying correct brings good fortune. After revolution, those who are not genuinely transformed must be watched carefully.

Luck Level: Caution
